This is a literal illustration of an event-driven system. Every click is a produced event. In batched mode it sits in a queue for 3 seconds before a consumer resolves it against whatever duck is actually there when it consumes — not the one you clicked on. That's why leading a duck (clicking ahead of its flight path) can land a hit even in batched mode: you're predicting future state instead of reacting to stale state. In real-time mode the event is processed the instant it's produced, against current state. Priority ducks (gold ring, faster, smaller target) are worth 3× the points, and a hit streak builds a score multiplier that resets on a miss — both reward staying accurate under pressure rather than just clicking fast.
